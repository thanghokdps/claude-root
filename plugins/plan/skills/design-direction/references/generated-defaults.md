# Generated defaults — what to check a design plan against

The looks a model reaches for whatever the subject is. None is wrong on its own: a brief can ask for any of them, and the brief wins. Each is a problem only when it fills an axis the brief left free, because then the product looks like every other generated product. Adapted from anthropics/skills `frontend-design` (Apache-2.0), addyosmani/agent-skills `frontend-ui-engineering` (MIT) and expo/skills `native-slop` (MIT); reworded, nothing copied.

## Palette

- Indigo or violet primary with a gradient behind the hero.
- Warm cream ground with a clay or terracotta accent and a high-contrast serif.
- Near-black ground with one acid-green or vermilion accent.
- "Trust blue" picked because the product is SaaS, finance or health.

## Type

- One geometric sans for everything, with no decision behind it.
- A serif headline used only to look premium.
- Monospace for small labels that are not code or data.
- A tracked-out all-caps label above every heading.
- One word of a headline accented in italic or colour.

## Structure

- Content chopped into identical rounded cards with one radius and one soft shadow.
- A column of same-sized stat tiles as the first thing on a dashboard.
- Numbered markers (01 / 02 / 03) on content that is not a sequence.
- Equal generous padding at every level, so section gaps match row gaps.
- A broadsheet layout of hairline rules with zero radius.

## Motion

- Fade-and-slide on every section, replayed on every visit.
- A hover lift on every card.

## Copy

- Meta strings joined with middle dots, or labels built as "Word — fragment".
- An arrow appended to every link and button.
- Lorem ipsum or invented stats where real content would show the layout's limits.

## How to use this list

For each axis the brief leaves free, ask: would I have chosen this for an unrelated product? If yes, replace it with something that comes from the subject's own world (its materials, tools, vocabulary, the place it is used) and say what changed and why. Do not fix a default by swapping it for another item on this list.
